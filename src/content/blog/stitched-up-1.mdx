# Stitched Up: The Beginning

Stitched Up is a narrative-driven psychological game centered around Silis, a 16-year-old boy caught in the quiet chaos of his own mind. At its core, the game is about identity, avoidance, and emotional deterioration, told through surreal, dreamlike sequences and emotionally driven choices.

![Stitched Up Banner](/imgs/blogImgs/stitchedup.png)

## PREMISE

This game aims to tell the story of a 16-year-old boy suffering with depression, identity crisis, anxiety, and a slouth of personal problems that overtake his world and the world surrounding him. Based in a modified version of the early 2000s, Stitched Up is a 'choices matter' game, each of which influencing the path each player takes.1
