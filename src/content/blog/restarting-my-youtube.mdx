## What do you mean "Restarting my YouTube"?

I first began my first YouTube channel almost 13 years ago, and since then, have dreamed of creating content that I am passionate about and that aligns with my character. Since then, I have gone through numreous variations of who I identify myself as: from freelance video editing, clips of my gameplay, comedy commentaries, to finally cutting myself off and rebranding.

## What's the Rebrand?

Now, I will be rebranding to focus on gaming content from time to time, but predominantly focus on vlog style content. As I finish my current video (title TBD), I look forward to an enlightening future of content creation. I have fallen back in love with the ability to create and the ability to show the world who I am.

I am passionate about the future of our content, and hope to display such to viewers.

## What should viewers expect?

Viewers should expect content that focuses on me and the situations I surround myself in. As I have relocated to the northern California area, I stride to film my day to day actions, influencing others to step out of the confort zone and challenge who they are as a person. I am a large advocate for mental health, and have always pushed to better myself and stay true to who I identify as "CAMERON" (or Camry).
